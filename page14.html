QUIRE

The uses of the word 'QUIRE'

  • Refers to two dozen (24) sheets of paper.
  • Historically used in bookbinding and papermaking.
  • Common in older literary and printing contexts.
  • Precursor to the modern term "ream" for paper quantities.
  • Can be used metaphorically to describe bundles or collections.
