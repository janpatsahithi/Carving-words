PETRICHOR

The uses of the word 'PETRICHOR'

  • Describes the earthy scent after fresh rainfall on dry soil.
  • Often used in poetic or literary contexts to evoke mood or nostalgia.
  • Popular in romantic descriptions of nature or the monsoon season.
  • Appears in environmental and meteorological discussions.
  • Rarely spoken in casual conversation but known by many readers.
