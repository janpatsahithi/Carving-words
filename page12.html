DEFENESTRATION

The uses of the word 'DEFENESTRATION'

  • Literally means throwing someone or something out of a window.
  • Used figuratively to mean removing someone from power or office.
  • Appears in historical and political contexts (e.g., Prague Defenestrations).
  • Can be used humorously to describe extreme disagreement or rejection.
  • Rarely used in casual conversation but sometimes in satire or dark humor.
