ULTRACREPIDARIAN

The uses of the word 'ULTRACREPIDARIAN'

  • Describes a person who offers opinions beyond their knowledge.
  • Used to call out uninformed commentary.
  • Common in academic, professional, or political discussions.
  • Originates from Latin meaning "beyond the sandal".
  • Useful in debates and discussions about credibility.
