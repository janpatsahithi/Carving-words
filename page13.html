JIRBLE

The uses of the word 'JIRBLE'

  • Means to spill (a liquid) by shaking or unsteady handling.
  • Very archaic and rare; practically unused in modern English.
  • Mostly found in historical or whimsical contexts.
  • Can be used humorously to describe clumsy pouring.
  • Appears in old dictionaries or literature from the 18th–19th century.
