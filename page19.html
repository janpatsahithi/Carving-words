TYROTOXISM

The uses of the word 'TYROTOXISM'

  • Describes poisoning caused by cheese or dairy products
  • Rarely used in modern medical terminology
  • Appears in historical medical literature
  • Used metaphorically in humorous or exaggerated contexts
  • Can refer to allergic reactions to dairy
