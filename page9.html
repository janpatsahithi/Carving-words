The uses of the word 'ZUGZWANG'

  • Used in chess to describe a situation where every move is disadvantageous.
  • Used metaphorically to describe being forced into a no-win decision.
  • Common in game theory discussions.
  • Appears in psychological and philosophical contexts.
  • Used in competitive and strategic scenarios in literature and media.
