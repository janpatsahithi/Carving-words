GRIFFONAGE

The uses of the word 'GRIFFONAGE'

  • Used to describe illegible or sloppy handwriting
  • Often referenced when discussing rushed notes
  • Can be used humorously to describe poor penmanship
  • Associated with hastily scribbled text or graffiti
  • Sometimes used in academic or literary critique
